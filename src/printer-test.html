What the result means

Greys look yellow, tan or brownCyan (and possibly magenta) is low or a nozzle is blocked. This is what your brochure prints show.
Greys look pink or redCyan is low.
Greys look greenMagenta is low.
Greys look blue or purpleYellow is low.
A patch is faint, streaky or missingRun the printer's head cleaning, then a nozzle check. Replace the cartridge if it does not recover after two cleans.
Everything looks right here but the brochure still looks offCheck the print settings: paper type should match your paper, quality High, and any "ink saver" or "draft" mode switched off.
