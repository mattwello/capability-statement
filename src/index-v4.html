Who we are
A Local Business. Built on Strong Foundations.

Westcoast Energy Electrical & Solar is a Perth-based electrical contracting business founded by long-time friends and electricians Matthew Wellstead and Mark Porges.

Built on a shared commitment to quality workmanship, clear communication and genuine customer service, Westcoast Energy brings a personal approach to professional electrical services.

We understand the demands placed on property managers — from coordinating access with tenants to managing maintenance requests, safety requirements and owner expectations.

Our approach is straightforward: deliver dependable electrical solutions, communicate clearly throughout the process and make managing property maintenance easier.
